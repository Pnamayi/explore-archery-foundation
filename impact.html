Our Theory Of Change

How opportunity becomes impact

01

Exposure creates possibility

A young person cannot pursue an opportunity they have never encountered.

02

Opportunity creates experience

Giving them access allows them to discover what interests them.

03

Experience builds skills

Practice, mentorship and participation transform interest into ability.

04

Skills build confidence

As young people become capable, they begin believing in their own potential.

05

Confidence creates action

Confident young people are more prepared to take responsibility and pursue opportunities.

06

Action creates impact

Young people who grow become contributors to their families, schools and communities.

Ultimate outcome: young people equipped to build better futures for themselves and others.
