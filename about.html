About Us

Not just an archery organization. A youth-development foundation.

We use archery and other practical experiences as tools for empowerment and opportunity — because archery is our starting point, but young people are our mission.

Our Big Idea

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity isn't.

Explore Archery Foundation exists to close that gap. We believe every young person has potential, but potential needs exposure, guidance, resources, encouragement and opportunity to become something meaningful.

PURPOSE

To create opportunities that help young people discover, develop and use their potential.

Discovering talents they didn't know they had. Developing practical and personal skills. Building confidence and discipline. Experiencing competition, creativity and teamwork. Meeting positive mentors. Exploring education and career possibilities. Taking responsibility and developing leadership. Contributing positively to their communities.

VISION

A future where every young person has the opportunity to discover their potential.

Our vision goes beyond archery. We want to contribute to a generation of young people who are confident, skilled, curious, resilient and capable of creating positive change.

MISSION

To create accessible opportunities for young people through archery, sport, STEM, education, mentorship and life-skills development.

Helping them discover their potential, build confidence and develop skills for life — through practical experiences rather than simply telling young people what they can become.

Our Overall Philosophy

Discover → Develop → Experience → Lead

01

Discover

"What am I capable of?"

Through sport, archery, STEM, creativity and new experiences, young people discover interests, strengths and talents.

02

Develop

"How can I become better?"

Through training, mentorship, education and consistent practice, potential begins becoming ability.

03

Experience

"What can I do with my skills?"

Competitions, projects, exhibitions, challenges and real-world experiences turn knowledge into confidence.

04

Lead

"How can I help someone else?"

We encourage young people to become responsible teammates, mentors, leaders and positive members of their communities.

We don't just teach a sport. We build potential.
Our Core Belief

An archery lesson can teach focus. A competition can teach resilience. A STEM project can teach problem-solving. A mentor can provide direction. A team can teach belonging. A leadership opportunity can build confidence.
